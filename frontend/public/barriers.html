Barrier Definitions

Cognitive friction arising from perceived misalignment between price point and utility. Often triggers "comparative browsing" behavior.

Structural lack of confidence in the physical or operational integrity of the product. Common in Fresh Produce and High-end Electronics.

Friction caused by uncertainty regarding last-mile logistics, timing, or the physical state of arrival.

Usability hurdles within the digital ecosystem that impede the transition from selection to checkout.
